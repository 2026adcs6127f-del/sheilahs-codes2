MURCHISON FALLS ADVENTURE

3 Days/ 2 Nights. Wild Safari

Murchison Falls National Park is one of Uganda’s best-known natural attractions. It is famous for the powerful waterfall formed where the Victoria Nile forces its way through a narrow rocky gorge, as well as its savannah landscapes, riverine habitats and diverse wildlife. The park offers visitors opportunities for game drives, boat cruises, birdwatching, photography and guided nature experiences.
